Build a premium, mobile-first, single scrolling page using your supplied section order and copy.

- Dark navy and charcoal styling, electric-blue booking buttons, green accents, Plus Jakarta Sans typography, and subtle motion.
- Sticky navigation and mobile menu; all six links smoothly scroll to their sections.
- Complete hero, pain points, six services, three-step process, six project cards with case-study dialogs, four testimonials, and three interactive industry tabs.
- Three pricing plans, five expandable FAQs, animated stats, final booking invitation, validated contact form, and complete footer.
